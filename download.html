Getting started

1

Download and install

Run the file you downloaded and follow the steps.

2

Enter your licence key

It arrives by email after you sign up. Paste it when LedgerPush asks.

3

Open TallyPrime and connect

Keep Tally open on the same computer. LedgerPush shows “Tally : Connected” at the top.

4

Read your first bill

Add a bill, check the details, and push it to Tally. Reading bills needs the internet.
