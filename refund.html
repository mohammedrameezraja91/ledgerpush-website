Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy applies to payments made to A.L. Mohammed Rameez Raja, Erode, Tamil Nadu, India (“LedgerPush”, “we”) for LedgerPush licences and credits.

1. Credits

  • Unused credits can be refunded if you ask within 7 days of payment. Credits already used are not refundable.
  • Credits do not expire.
  • Free starter credits, including those that come with a trial, have no cash value and are not refundable.
  • A credit is charged only after a bill is read successfully. If a credit is charged by mistake, for example because of a technical fault, write to us and we will restore it.

2. Trial

The trial payment made with an invite code (for example ₹1) is a nominal fee to start the trial and is not refundable. You can stop using LedgerPush at any time.

3. Licence fees

The refund terms for paid licences will be shown to you at the time of purchase.

4. Cancelling

You can stop at any time. Your licence stays active until its end date. We do not charge you automatically.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email ledgerpush.admin@gmail.com with your licence key and the UPI transaction ID of your payment. We reply within 3 working days. Approved refunds are sent back to the account you paid from within 7 working days.
