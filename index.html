Questions, answered.

Does it post to Tally by itself?

No. You review every entry, pick the cost centre and push it.

What if a bill can’t be read?

It still arrives in its window, empty, with a note to fill it from the photo.

Do I need new hardware?

No. Your Tally PC, a small Connector app and an Android phone are enough.

Does it work for several branches?

Yes. Each branch has its own login, and bills are filed per branch.

How LedgerPush uses your Google data

LedgerPush asks for Google access only for the cash book import feature, and only if you choose to use it. When you click Sign in with Google, LedgerPush requests:

  • Your Google account email address — only to show you which Google account is connected.
  • Read-only access to Google Sheets — to read the cash book spreadsheet whose link you paste into LedgerPush. It cannot edit or delete anything.

The cash book entries it reads are saved on your own computer and used only to create entries in your Tally. Your Google data is never sent to our servers, never shared or sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI. You can disconnect at any time in LedgerPush’s Settings, or from your Google Account permissions.

LedgerPush’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
